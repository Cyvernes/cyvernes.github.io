About

I am a PhD student at Université Grenoble Alpes, in the APTIKAL team of the Grenoble Informatics Laboratory (LIG). My work focuses on causal inference when the causal structure is only partially known, for instance through a summary graph or an abstraction. More specifically, I study the identification of causal effects: determining when the effect of an intervention can be computed from observed data.

PhD

PhD in computer science and applied mathematics, Université Grenoble Alpes (APTIKAL, LIG), ongoing since 2024. Supervisors: Éric Gaussier, Marianne Clausel and Émilie Devijver.
